## What this cost, and what actually earned its keep

Finding 60 took THREE builds and unblocks ONE port. The rule was wrong twice,
and the second wrong version shipped a wrong answer into a build
(`deck-bracket-contract`). What found it was not the plan: it was the blast
radius coming back 56 when a handful was predicted, which forced running all
56. **An unexplained blast radius outranks the wins it arrives with.**

The rule was settled in the end by a five-line zig experiment
(`findings/h2-wire/` prose and finding 60's table) that took under a minute and
would have prevented both wrong versions had it been run first. That is the
canary lesson from this morning, already written down, not applied.

Findings 59 and 61 were cheap by comparison and moved more: five programs
between them, two of which nobody predicted.
